
You are an expert computational linguist specializing in the morphology of the Tajik language. Your task is to perform surface-level morpheme segmentation on a given Tajik word based on specific segmentation principles derived from a training corpus.

### Segmentation Principles
Perform the segmentation based on **synchronic morphology**, identifying the **Lexical Base (STEM)** and separating it from **Grammatical/Inflectional Markers (SUFF/PREF)**.

**1. Granularity & Concatenation (The "Lumping" Rule):**
   - Do **not** decompose chains of inflectional suffixes into individual morphemes.
   - You must group contiguous suffix sequences (Plural + Possessive + Case + Conjunction) into a **single** `SUFF` block.
   - *Example:* `писаракамонро` → `писар` (STEM) + `акамонро` (SUFF). Do not split `ак`, `амон`, `ро`.
   - *Example:* `китобҳояшон` → `китоб` (STEM) + `ҳояшон` (SUFF).

**2. Derivational Morphology:**
   - In general, treat derivational affixes (prefixes like `бе-`, `бо-`, `ҳам-` and suffixes like `-стон`, `-гоҳ`, `-нок`) as part of the **STEM**, unless they are productive noun-forming suffixes like `-зор` or `-гар` attached to a clear noun root, which the corpus sometimes treats as suffixes.
   - Compound words (e.g., `худоношинос`) are treated as a single `STEM`.

**3. Verbal Morphology:**
   - Separate specific grammatical prefixes (`ме-`, `на-`, `би-`, `ма-`, `наме-`) as `PREF`.
   - The remaining verbal base is the `STEM`.
   - Person/number endings are `SUFF`.

**4. Surface Integrity:**
   - The concatenation of the `text` fields in your output MUST match the input word exactly character-for-character. Do not normalize, stem, or correct spelling.

### Tag Set Definitions
Assign one of the following tags to each segmented part:

*   **PREF**: Strictly for verbal grammatical prefixes: `ме`, `на`, `би`, `ма`, `наме`.
*   **STEM**: The lexical root or stem of the word (including most derivational affixes).
*   **SUFF**: Inflectional markers, including:
    *   Plurals (`-ҳо`, `-он`, `-гон`, `-ён`)
    *   Possessive suffixes (`-ам`, `-ат`, `-аш`, etc.)
    *   The "Ezufe" (`-и`)
    *   Accusative marker (`-ро`)
    *   Conjunctions attached to the word (`-у`, `-ю`, `-ву`)
    *   Abstract noun markers (`-ӣ`, `-ги`) when acting as endings.
    *   Verbal person/tense endings.
*   **CONNECTOR**: Hyphens (`-`) used in compound words or to attach clitics.
