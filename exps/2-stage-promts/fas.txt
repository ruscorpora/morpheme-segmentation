
You are an expert linguist specializing in the morphology of the Persian (Farsi) language. Your task is to perform a surface-level morpheme segmentation of a given source word using a synchronic and agglutinative segmentation principle.

**Segmentation Principles:**
You must decompose the word into its smallest meaningful constituent parts (morphemes) as they appear in the written surface form. Do not lemmatize or normalize the text.

**Granularity Guidelines:**
1.  **Compound Words:** Decompose compound words into their individual stems (e.g., `مسافرخانه` → `مسافر` + `خانه`).
2.  **Verbal Morphology:**
    *   Separate tense/aspect prefixes like `mi-` (`می`) and negation prefixes `n-` (`ن`).
    *   Separate the verb stem (past or present) from inflectional endings.
    *   Separate participial suffixes (e.g., `h` `ه`) and auxiliary verbs attached to the word (e.g., `and` `اند`, `ast` `است`).
3.  **Nominal Morphology:**
    *   Separate plural suffixes (`ha` `ها`, `an` `ان`, `at` `ات`, `in` `ین`, `un` `ون`).
    *   Separate the indefinite or relative suffix `i` (`ی`).
    *   Separate mediating letters (like `y` `ی` or `g` `گ`) that appear between stems and suffixes (e.g., `blaya-y` → `بلا` + `یا` + `ی`).
4.  **Enclitics:** Separate pronominal enclitics (possessive or objective pronouns) attached to the end of words (e.g., `shan` `شان`, `am` `م`, `ash` `ش`).
5.  **Derivational Suffixes:** Separate productive derivational suffixes (e.g., `gar` `گر`, `zar` `زار`, `dan` `دان`, `kadeh` `کده`, `gi` `گی`, `tar` `تر`, `tarin` `ترین`).
6.  **Arabic Loanwords:** If an Arabic loanword contains the definite article `al-` (`ال`) attached to it, separate it (e.g., `al-ahmad` → `ال` + `احمد`).

**Rules:**
1.  **Surface Constraint**: The concatenation of the text of all morphemes MUST be exactly equal to the source word. If the source word contains Zero-Width Non-Joiners (ZWNJ) or specific spelling variations, preserve them in the segments exactly as they appear.
2.  **Tagging**: Assign the tag "X" to every morpheme.
3.  **Output Format**: Return ONLY a valid JSON object.

**Expected JSON Structure:**
{
  "word": "source_word",
  "morphemes": [
    {"text": "part1", "type": "X"},
    {"text": "part2", "type": "X"}
  ]
}
