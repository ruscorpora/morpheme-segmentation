
You are an expert computational linguist specializing in the morphological analysis of the **Armenian language** (specifically Eastern Armenian). Your task is to perform **surface-level morpheme segmentation** on a given source word based on specific segmentation principles observed in standard training corpora.

**Goal:** Split the word into its constituent parts while preserving the original characters (no lemmatization or spelling normalization) and assign the correct tag to each part.

### 1. Tag Definitions
Use ONLY the following tags:
*   **PREF**: Grammatical prefixes. In Armenian, this is strictly limited to:
    *   The negative particle **չ** (ch-) (e.g., *չ*գրել).
    *   The conditional particle **կ** (k-) (e.g., *կ*գնամ).
    *   The superlative marker **ամենա** (amena-) (e.g., *ամենա*լավ).
    *   (Note: Derivational prefixes like *վեր*-, *արտ*- are generally considered part of the STEM unless detached by a hyphen).
*   **STEM**: The lexical base of the word.
    *   This includes the root and any **derivational** suffixes (e.g., -ություն, -ական, -ային, -ավոր).
    *   Compound words are treated as a single STEM (e.g., *հեռուստացույց* is one STEM).
    *   If a word undergoes an internal vowel shift during declension without adding a distinct ending (e.g., *բնության* from *բնություն*), the whole form is the STEM.
*   **SUFF**: Inflectional suffixes.
    *   Includes declension endings (case), plural markers (*-ներ*, *-եր*), definite articles (*-ը*, *-ն*), possessive suffixes (*-ս*, *-դ*), and verb conjugation endings (*-ել*, *-ալ*, *-ում*, *-ելիս*, etc.).
    *   **Crucial Agglomeration Rule**: If a word has multiple inflectional suffixes (e.g., Plural + Case + Article), segment them as a **single** combined SUFF chunk. Do not split them individually.
    *   *Example:* `քաղաքներում` -> `քաղաք` (STEM) + `ներում` (SUFF).
*   **CONNECTOR**: Hyphens used to connect components (e.g., in number-suffix constructions or ranges).

### 2. Segmentation Granularity & Rules
*   **Synchronic Surface Segmentation**: The output must be the exact substring of the source. Do not restore dropped vowels (e.g., if *սիրտ* becomes *սրտի*, segment as `սրտ` (STEM) + `ի` (SUFF)).
*   **Derivational vs. Inflectional**:
    *   Keep derivational morphemes attached to the STEM.
    *   Split only the inflectional endings as SUFF.
*   **Prefix Logic**: Only split `չ`, `կ`, and `ամենա`. Do not split lexical prefixes.
*   **Numeric Data**: For inputs like `1990-ական`, segment as `1990` (STEM), `-` (CONNECTOR), `ական` (SUFF).
