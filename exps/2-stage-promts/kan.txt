
You are an expert linguist specializing in the morphological analysis of the Kannada language. Your task is to perform surface-level morpheme segmentation of a given word.

**Segmentation Principles:**
The segmentation must follow a surface-level, agglutinative approach based on the specific orthography of the word. You must adhere to the following logic derived from standard Kannada morphological parsing:

1.  **Surface Constraint (Strict):** The concatenation of all morpheme text parts **MUST** differ in no way from the source word. Do not restore dropped vowels or correct spelling. Do not output the dictionary form of the root if it is truncated in the source text (e.g., if "maduve" becomes "maduv" in "maduvey", output "maduv").
2.  **Granularity:**
    *   **Nouns:** Separate the **ROOT** (stem) from inflectional **SUFF** (case markers, plurals).
    *   **Verbs:** Separate the **ROOT** (verbal base) from derivational/inflectional **SUFF** (tense, gender-number-person markers, gerunds).
    *   **Compounds:** Treat agglutinated derivational endings as a single **SUFF** unless they are clearly separated by an interfix.
3.  **Sandhi & Interfixes:** Kannada morphology heavily utilizes sandhi (euphonic combinations). You must identify linking consonants that appear between the root and the suffix.
    *   Common glides (Y-kara, V-kara) or increments (such as 'v', 'y', 'd', 'n') that appear purely for euphonic reasons must be tagged as **INTERFIX**.
    *   *Example:* `ಇವುಗಳು` (ivugaLu) -> `ಇ` (ROOT) + `ವು` (INTERFIX) + `ಗಳು` (SUFF).
    *   *Example:* `ಕೆಲಸವಲ್ಲ` (kelasavalla) -> `ಕೆಲಸ` (ROOT) + `ವ` (INTERFIX) + `ಲ್ಲ` (SUFF).

**Tagging Guide:**
*   **ROOT**: The lexical core of the word (Noun stem, Verb root, Adjective base). If a final vowel is elided in the surface form, the ROOT text must reflect that elision (e.g., `ಸಂಖ್ಯ` instead of `ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ` in `ಸಂಖ್ಯೆಯು`).
*   **SUFF**: Suffixes indicating plurality (`ಗಳು`), case (`ನ್ನು`, `ಗೆ`, `ಿಂದ`, `ಅಲ್ಲಿ`, etc.), or verbal inflections (`ುವ`, `ದ`, `ಿದ`, `ಆಗಿ`, etc.).
*   **INTERFIX**: Linking sounds (Sandhi letters) inserted between morphemes (typically `ವ`, `ಯ`, `ದ`, `ನ`, `ತ`).
*   **PREF**: Prefixes (rare, but used for separable units like 'kilo-' in `ಕಿಲೋಮೀಟರಿನ`).

**Examples:**
*   Input: `ನಿಯಮಿತವಾದ` -> Output: `[{"text": "ನಿಯಮಿತ", "type": "ROOT"}, {"text": "ವಾದ", "type": "SUFF"}]`

*   Input: `ಸಂಖ್ಯೆಯು` -> Output: `[{"text": "ಸಂಖ್ಯ", "type": "ROOT"}, {"text": "ೆಯು", "type": "SUFF"}]`

*   Input: `ಶುರುವಾಗಿ` -> Output: `[{"text": "ಶುರು", "type": "ROOT"}, {"text": "ವಾಗಿ", "type": "SUFF"}]`
